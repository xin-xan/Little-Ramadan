# Little Ramadan — Privacy notice

_Draft for owner and legal review before public launch (CLAUDE.md §11.2: review Maldives and other launch-market requirements)._

**What Little Ramadan is:** a free educational web app for children aged 7–9. It has no accounts and no servers that receive children's data.

**Information stored:** a nickname and a cartoon avatar; app settings (sound, motion, calendar dates, optional journal on/off, break reminder, optional parent PIN stored as a salted hash); completed activities, badges, decorations and goals; optional reflections and optional fasting-journal entries; the optional Salah tracker (which of today's five prayers were marked, kept for 60 days); optional parent voice recordings.

**Where:** only on the device, in the browser's IndexedDB storage.

**What leaves the device:** nothing. No analytics, advertising, tracking cookies, social logins or third-party services. The app is a static website; the hosting provider can see standard technical request logs (such as IP address) when the app files are downloaded, as with any website. These logs contain no profile data.

**Never collected:** real names, dates of birth, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, school, location, contacts, photos or camera access.

**Parent voice recordings (optional):** in Parents → Voice recordings, a grown-up can record their own voice reading stories, lessons, quiz questions and Dhivehi letters. The browser asks for microphone permission the first time. The microphone is on only while the grown-up is recording, and children are never asked to record. Recordings are saved only on this device, separately from progress. They are never uploaded. "Reset progress" keeps them; "Delete everything" or "Delete all recordings" removes them. A parent can save a backup file, which stays with them.

**Retention and deletion:** data stays until the parent deletes it (Parents → Reset & delete), clears the browser's data, or removes the app.

**Children's safety:** no chat, messaging, public profiles, leaderboards, user-generated public content, external links in the children's area, advertising or purchases.
