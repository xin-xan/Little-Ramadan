# Little Ramadan — Parent guide

Little Ramadan is a free Ramadan learning adventure for children aged 7–9. There are no ads, purchases, accounts or chat, and everything stays on your device.

## Getting started
1. Open the app link in Safari (iPhone/iPad) or Chrome (Android).
2. Add it to your home screen (see `docs/install.md`) so it opens like an app and works offline.
3. Tap **Start**, then hand over when asked for a grown-up. Answer the grown-up question and read the privacy summary.
4. Your child picks a nickname (not their real name) and a sea-creature avatar.

## Setting Ramadan dates
Parents → **Calendar**. Enter the first day of Ramadan from your local official announcement and choose 29 or 30 days. Not sure yet? Choose 30 and switch to 29 if Eid is announced early; nothing is lost.
Without dates, your child sees "Explore Ramadan" with every day open. Days never lock again once open, and missed days are never punished. You can also choose **Open all journey days now**.

## What your child does each day
A short story or lesson, one real-life kindness mission ("I did it!"), and a gentle game or quiz. Optional private reflections. A full day takes about 10–15 minutes, and the app suggests a natural stopping point afterwards.

## Stars, badges and the room
Stars come only from learning, kindness, games and goals. They are never taken away and are never linked to fasting. Badges and room decorations unlock from the same activities.

## Fasting
- The app never rewards or counts fasting and has no streaks or timers.
- **My First Fast** explains fasting gently. Children are always told that their health comes first and stopping is okay.
- The optional private journal is **off** until you turn it on (Parents → Fasting journal). You can delete entries at any time.
- The app is not medical advice. Please talk to a health professional about whether your child is ready to practise fasting.

## Parent Zone
Open **Parents** in the bottom menu. By default it asks a grown-up maths question (this keeps young children out but is not security). You can set a 4–6 digit PIN under Settings; it is stored only as a secure hash.
From there you can see progress and reflections, change sound and motion, set a gentle break reminder, read the privacy notice and content review status, reset progress or delete everything.

## Record your own voice
In **Parents → Voice recordings** you can read stories, lessons, quiz questions, kindness missions and Dhivehi letters aloud in your own voice. Tap **Record**, read the words shown, then tap **Stop**. Your child will see a **Listen** button on everything you have recorded; anything you haven't recorded stays as text. You don't need to record everything — start with a favourite story or the Dhivehi letter sounds.

The phone will ask for microphone permission the first time. Recordings stay only on this phone. Use **Save a backup** to keep a copy, and **Load a backup** to restore it (for example on a new phone). "Reset progress" keeps your recordings; "Delete everything" removes them.

## Important to know
- Progress is saved only in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data, uninstalling, or switching devices or browsers will lose it.
- The religious-learning content is being reviewed by qualified reviewers. Please guide your child with your family's own teachers and scholars.

## Learn Dhivehi
Open **Learn Dhivehi** from the home screen. Each day of Ramadan has one short activity: days 1 to 24 introduce a new Thaana letter (with a word, a picture and a chance to trace it), then come review days, and the last day is about Eid words. If you have set your Ramadan dates, each day opens on its date; missed days stay open. All 12 games can also be played any time. The Dhivehi words are shown in Thaana with a romanised spelling underneath; the app's English voice reads the instructions and the romanised words, so please help with true Dhivehi pronunciation.
